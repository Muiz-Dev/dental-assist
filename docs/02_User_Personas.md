# 1. Introduction

This document defines the primary and secondary users of DentAssist.

Its purpose is to ensure that every feature is designed around real user needs rather than technical preferences.

When making product decisions, always ask:

> "Which persona does this feature help?"

If the answer is "none," the feature should be reconsidered.

---

# 2. Product Design Principles

DentAssist should always feel:

- Calm
- Trustworthy
- Professional
- Friendly
- Educational
- Honest
- Reassuring

The application should never create unnecessary fear or make users feel judged.

# Persona 1

## Worried Adult

### Profile

Age:

20–55

Technology Experience:

Medium

Primary Device:

Mobile Phone

Typical Situation:

The user notices unexpected dental symptoms and wants to understand what they might mean before deciding whether to visit a dentist.

Examples:

"My gum is bleeding."

"My tooth hurts."

"My filling came out."

"My jaw feels swollen."

---

### Goals

Understand symptoms.

Know whether the issue may require urgent attention.

Reduce anxiety.

Locate a nearby dentist if necessary.

---

### Pain Points

Too much conflicting information online.

Fear of expensive treatment.

Uncertainty about urgency.

Medical terminology is difficult to understand.

---

### Success Criteria

The user leaves feeling:

- calmer,
- better informed,
- aware of next steps,
- encouraged to seek professional care when needed.

# Persona 2

## Parent

### Profile

Age:

25–50

Children:

1 or more

Primary Concern:

Children's oral health.

---

Examples

"My child chipped a tooth."

"My baby is teething."

"My child refuses to brush."

---

Goals

Learn what is normal.

Understand warning signs.

Find child-friendly dentists.

---

Pain Points

Children cannot always explain pain.

Parents worry about making the wrong decision.

---

Desired Experience

Simple explanations.

Practical advice.

Gentle language.

---

Easy explanations.

# Persona 4

## Traveler

Situation

The user is away from home and develops a dental problem.

Goals

Find nearby dentists quickly.

Understand whether treatment can wait.

Access emergency guidance.

---

Pain Points

Does not know local clinics.

May not speak the local language.

---

Desired Experience

Fast location results.

Simple directions.

Minimal account setup.

---

# Persona 5

## Routine User

Situation

They simply want healthier oral habits.

---

Goals

Schedule dental checkups.

Learn prevention.

Track oral care.

---

Desired Experience

Positive reinforcement.

Helpful reminders.

Educational tips.

# User Goals

Across all personas, users generally want to:

- Understand symptoms.
- Reduce uncertainty.
- Find reliable information.
- Discover nearby care.
- Avoid unnecessary panic.
- Protect their oral health.

---

# Common User Frustrations

Users dislike:

Long registration forms.

Medical jargon.

Very long AI responses.

Conflicting advice.

Slow loading screens.

Repeated questions.

Being forced to create an account immediately.

---

# Accessibility Needs

Some users may have:

Poor eyesight.

Low health literacy.

Limited internet connectivity.

Limited technical knowledge.

The application should therefore support:

Large text.

High contrast.

Simple navigation.

Readable language.

Fast loading on slower networks.

---

# Emotional States

Most users are not opening DentAssist because they are curious.

They are opening it because something is wrong.

Possible emotions include:

Anxiety.

Fear.

Pain.

Embarrassment.

Stress.

Uncertainty.

The application should acknowledge these emotions without making assumptions.

Example:

Instead of saying:

"This is serious."

Prefer:

"I understand dental discomfort can be worrying. Based on what you've shared, here's some educational information and when it would be appropriate to seek professional care."

---

# AI Communication Guidelines

The AI should:

Use short paragraphs.

Avoid unexplained medical terminology.

Explain technical words when they are necessary.

Avoid absolute certainty.

Never shame users.

Never guess.

Always distinguish between:

Facts

Possibilities

Recommendations

---

# Reading Level

Target reading level:

Approximately 8th-grade level.

Users should not need medical training to understand the responses.

# UX Principles Derived from Personas

Based on these personas, the product should follow these design rules:

1. Let users ask a question within seconds of opening the app.

2. Delay account creation until the user sees value.

3. Prefer guided questions over large text forms when possible.

4. Always end AI responses with clear next steps.

5. Make "Find a Dentist" available from every major screen.

6. Clearly explain that the AI provides educational information and is not a replacement for professional care.

7. Preserve conversation context so users do not need to repeat themselves.

8. Design for reassurance rather than alarm.

---

# Definition of Success

A successful interaction is one where the user finishes the conversation feeling:

- Better informed.
- Less anxious.
- Aware of appropriate next steps.
- More confident about seeking professional care when needed.
